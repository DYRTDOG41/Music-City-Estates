5. AI Engineer — Mix My Song

Choose the sound you want. Music City cleans the lead vocal with ElevenLabs when available, then automatically applies track-aware EQ, compression, stereo placement, reverb, delay, gain staging and master-bus control.

Lower = natural and dry. Higher = more obvious space, width and vocal effects.

Record your lead, doubles and ad-libs, then let the engineer take over.

6. Finished Record — A/B Mixes

Version A stays cleaner and more natural. Version B pushes the creative effects harder. Pick the mix that fits your song.

Advanced tools are optional

Most players do not need a mixing console to make their first song. Open the advanced mixer only when you want hands-on track controls.
